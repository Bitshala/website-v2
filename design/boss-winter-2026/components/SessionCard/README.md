One session: a white time chip, a bold Inter title, an italic "by" line and the duration in the bottom-right corner. This is the Summer 2026 card.

`BOSS.html.sessionCard(session, { open })`.

- **Kinds:** a `workshop` is olive with a yellow `olive-line` edge, a `talk` is `slate`, and an `open` stage slot is `plum-800` with a `plum-500` edge.
- **Titles with a colon:** the part after the first `: ` drops to a smaller subtitle line, as in "Epoch Shield: / End-to-End Encrypted Messaging on Nostr".
- **Speakers:** several speakers are joined as "A, B & C". An open-stage slot has no "by" line.
- **Abstracts:** a card with an `about` text is a native `<details>` with a small + by its duration. Tap or press Enter to read it.
